# Onboarding Checklist SOP for Northwind Systems

## Overview

The onboarding checklist ensures that new hires at Northwind Systems are quickly and effectively integrated into our company culture, processes, and technology. This document outlines the steps to be taken during the initial phase of a new employee's tenure, from their first day up to one month post-employment.

## Prerequisites

1. **Pre-Onboarding Information:**
   - New hire has completed all pre-onboarding tasks via our internal HR portal (https://hr.northwindsystems.com).
   - Personal details and information are available in the employee’s profile on our HRIS system, ADP OnBoard (https://adponboard.northwindsystems.com).

2. **Access Information:**
   - New hire has received a welcome email with login credentials for internal tools.
   - Login credentials include access to company email via Microsoft 365 (https://outlook.office.com), and internal network resources.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Day 1: Orientation and Setup

1. **Welcome Coffee and Badge Pickup:**
   - The new hire is greeted by the HR team, who provide a welcome pack containing company information, badge, and parking pass.
   
2. **Company Overview Presentation:**
   - Attend a virtual orientation session led by the HR team (Zoom URL: https://zoom.us/j/9876543210).
   - Topics include company history, mission, values, and key departments.

3. **Desk Setup and Equipment:**
   - The new hire is assigned a desk in the office.
   - IT department installs necessary software (e.g., Microsoft 365, Slack) on their provided laptop.
   - Provide a hard copy of the company’s internal security policies and guidelines.

### Day 2-7: Initial Integration

1. **IT Setup:**
   - New hire logs into Microsoft 365 with credentials from ADP OnBoard (https://adponboard.northwindsystems.com).
   - IT team configures their account for network access, email, and any required software licenses.

2. **Access to Internal Tools:**
   - Set up accounts on internal collaboration tools such as Microsoft Teams and Confluence.
     - Teams URL: https://teams.microsoft.com
     - Confluence URL: https://confluence.northwindsystems.com

3. **Training Sessions:**
   - Attend a training session provided by the IT department to familiarize with company’s technology stack (e.g., CRM, ERP systems).
   
4. **HR and Benefits Information:**
   - Review HR policies and benefits via ADP OnBoard.
   - Schedule a meeting with the HR manager for further details on benefits enrollment.

### Day 8-30: Full Integration

1. **Department Introduction:**
   - Attend an introductory session led by their department head or team lead.
   - Introduce them to key team members and provide access to necessary internal documentation and tools.

2. **Project Familiarization:**
   - Review ongoing projects they will be involved with via the project management tool, Jira (https://jira.northwindsystems.com).
   - Ensure they have the necessary background information on current tasks and responsibilities.

3. **Feedback Loop:**
   - Encourage new hires to provide feedback on their onboarding experience through a post-onboarding survey.
   - Contact details for HR support are provided during orientation, with an email address of hr.support@northwindsystems.com.

## Troubleshooting

### Technical Issues
- If the new hire encounters any technical issues (e.g., network connection problems), they should contact IT Support immediately via helpdesk.northwindsystems.com or by phone at 555-1234.
  
### HR Concerns
- For HR-related concerns, new hires can reach out to their assigned HR representative or the HR department at hr@northwindsystems.com.

### Example Scenario:
- **Issue:** New hire reports difficulty accessing internal network resources using their laptop.
  - **Resolution Steps:**
    1. The IT helpdesk is contacted via ticket submission at helpdesk.northwindsystems.com.
    2. A member of the IT support team verifies the issue and troubleshoots, likely resolving it within 30 minutes.

By following this onboarding checklist, new hires can be effectively integrated into Northwind Systems, ensuring a smooth transition from their first day to becoming productive members of our team.
