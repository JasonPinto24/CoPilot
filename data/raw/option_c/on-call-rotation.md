# Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for On-Call Rotation at Northwind Systems

## Overview

The on-call rotation system ensures that there is always someone available to handle critical incidents and support requests outside of regular working hours. This SOP outlines the process for managing on-call rotations, including scheduling, responsibilities, and communication procedures.

## Prerequisites

### Tools and Resources
- **OnCall Tool**: A web-based platform used for managing on-call schedules and incident responses (URL: https://oncall.northwind.com).
- **Slack Channels**: Dedicated channels such as #oncall and #support for real-time communication.
- **Incident Management System**: A tool like JIRA for logging, tracking, and resolving incidents (URL: https://jira.northwind.com).

### Team Structure
- **IT Support Team**: Comprises 10 members responsible for handling technical issues.
- **Management Team**: Includes the IT Manager and Deputy IT Manager who oversee on-call rotations.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Scheduling On-Call Rotations
1. **Weekly Planning**
   - The IT Manager will hold a weekly planning meeting (via Zoom) to finalize the next week's on-call schedule.
   - Each team member will be assigned an on-call period, typically one night per week.

2. **OnCall Tool Configuration**
   - Use the OnCall tool to create and manage schedules. Ensure that each on-call period is correctly set up with relevant information (e.g., contact details, time slots).
   - Example: "John Doe" will be on call from 7 PM to 7 AM on Wednesday, October 15.

3. **Notification Setup**
   - Configure the OnCall tool to send automated notifications via email and Slack channels for each on-call shift.
   - Example notification message: "John Doe is now on call from 7 PM to 7 AM."

### Handling Incidents
1. **Incident Notification**
   - Upon receiving an incident, the on-call team member should acknowledge the issue by responding in the designated Slack channel.
   - Example response: "Received incident report for critical server downtime."

2. **Documentation and Escalation**
   - Log the incident details in JIRA, including relevant information such as time of occurrence, severity level, and initial steps taken.
   - If the issue cannot be resolved within a reasonable timeframe (e.g., 30 minutes), escalate it to the next higher support team or management.

3. **Communication with Management**
   - In case of major incidents, notify the IT Manager via email and Slack immediately.
   - Example: "Critical database failure at 10 PM. Initial steps taken but unable to resolve; escalating."

### Post-Incident Follow-Up
1. **Review and Close Incident**
   - After resolving the incident, update JIRA with a resolution summary and any necessary actions.
   - Mark the incident as resolved in JIRA.

2. **Feedback Collection**
   - Request feedback from team members regarding their on-call experience through an internal survey tool (e.g., SurveyMonkey).
   - Use this feedback to continuously improve the on-call process and support procedures.

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues
1. **Missing or Incorrect On-Call Information**
   - Ensure that all team members are regularly updating their personal details in the OnCall tool.
   - Regularly review and confirm the accuracy of upcoming schedules before they take effect.

2. **Delayed Incident Responses**
   - Implement strict timelines for acknowledging incidents within 5 minutes and escalating major issues within 30 minutes.
   - Conduct regular training sessions to ensure all team members are familiar with incident response procedures.

3. **Communication Breakdowns**
   - Encourage the use of Slack for real-time communication and email for detailed reporting.
   - Establish clear escalation paths in case primary contacts are unavailable.
