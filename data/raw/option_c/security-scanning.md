# Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for Security Scanning at Northwind Systems

## Overview

Northwind Systems employs regular security scanning to identify and mitigate vulnerabilities in our systems and applications. This SOP outlines the process for conducting automated security scans using Nessus, a widely-used vulnerability scanner tool. The purpose of this document is to ensure consistent and efficient execution of the scanning process across all teams.

## Prerequisites

1. **Access**: Ensure you have an active account with the Nessus Security Scanner.
2. **Permissions**: Only authorized personnel from the IT Security team are allowed to perform security scans.
3. **Network Access**: Ensure that the Nessus scanner has proper network access and can reach all target hosts.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### 1. Preparation

1. **Log in to Nessus**:
   - Open a web browser and navigate to `https://nessus.northwind.com`.
   - Log in using your credentials provided by the IT Security team.
   
2. **Review Targets**: 
   - Go to the "Scans" tab on the left sidebar.
   - Review the list of targets (hosts, networks) that need scanning.

### 2. Creating a New Scan

1. **Create a New Scan**:
   - Click on the "New Scan" button in the top right corner.
   
2. **Define Scan Settings**:
   - **Name**: Provide a descriptive name for the scan (e.g., "Weekly Server Scan").
   - **Target**: Enter IP addresses, hostnames, or use predefined groups from your targets list.
   - **Plugins**: Select relevant plugins to ensure comprehensive coverage. Commonly used plugins include `All Plugin` and specific categories like `Web Applications`.
   - **Schedule**: Set a schedule for the scan if it’s a recurring task (e.g., "Weekly on Monday at 02:00").
   
3. **Save and Start Scan**:
   - Click "Start" to initiate the scan.
   - Monitor the scan status in the "Scans" tab.

### 3. Scanning Process

1. **Monitor Scan Progress**:
   - The status bar will indicate the progress of each target.
   - You can click on a specific host to view detailed results.
   
2. **Review Results**:
   - Once the scan is complete, review the report generated by Nessus.
   - Look for critical vulnerabilities and prioritize them based on severity.

### 4. Reporting and Follow-Up

1. **Generate Report**:
   - Click on "Download" to generate a detailed report in PDF or CSV format.
   - Share this report with relevant team members via email or internal sharing platforms like Sharepoint.
   
2. **Document Findings**:
   - Log any critical findings in the company’s incident management system (e.g., JIRA).
   - Assign remediation tasks to appropriate teams based on the severity and impact of vulnerabilities.

### 5. Archiving Completed Scans

1. **Archive Scan Data**:
   - Right-click on a completed scan in the "Scans" tab.
   - Select "Archive" to save the scan data for future reference.
   
2. **Clean Up Old Scans**:
   - Periodically review and archive or delete old scans that are no longer relevant.

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues and Solutions

1. **Failed Scan**:
   - **Issue**: Scan fails due to network connectivity issues.
     - **Solution**: Check the network settings of the Nessus scanner and ensure it has proper access to all targets.
   
2. **Slow Scans**:
   - **Issue**: Scans are taking longer than expected.
     - **Solution**: Optimize scan configurations by adjusting plugin selection or schedule scans during off-peak times.
   
3. **Missing Results**:
   - **Issue**: Some hosts are not showing up in the scan results.
     - **Solution**: Verify that the targets have open ports and Nessus has the correct credentials to access them.
