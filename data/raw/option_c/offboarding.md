# Offboarding SOP for Northwind Systems

## Overview
The offboarding process at Northwind Systems ensures that all employee data and access are properly managed when an employee leaves the company. This procedure covers the necessary steps to ensure data security, maintain compliance, and support ongoing operations. The Offboarding team is responsible for executing these procedures.

## Prerequisites
- Ensure the employee's manager or HR representative has initiated the offboarding process.
- Obtain a signed offboarding checklist from the employee’s supervisor.
- Verify that all necessary documentation (e.g., non-disclosure agreements) is in place.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### 1. Initial Notification and Documentation Collection
- **Contact Information**: 
  - Offboarding Team Lead: [OffboardingTeamLead@northwindsystems.com]
  - HR Representative: [HRRepresentative@northwindsystems.com]

- **Initial Steps**:
  - Send an email to the Offboarding Team Lead and HR representative with the employee's name, department, and confirmation of their last day.
  - Obtain a signed offboarding checklist from the employee’s supervisor.

### 2. Access Revocation
- **Tool Name**: SSO (Single Sign-On) Portal: <https://sso.northwindsystems.com>
- **Access Types**:
  - **Corporate Email and Calendar**: Use the SSO portal to revoke access.
    - Log in as Offboarding Team Lead.
    - Navigate to "User Management" > "Revoke Access".
    - Select the employee’s email address and confirm revocation.
  - **Internal Systems and Applications**: 
    - For each system, use the respective tool's SSO portal or admin panel to revoke access.
    - Example: Revoking access from the company’s CRM system:
      - Log in as Offboarding Team Lead.
      - Navigate to "CRM System" > "User Management".
      - Locate and remove the employee’s user account.

### 3. Data Transfer
- **Tool Name**: Data Transfer Tool: <https://data-transfer.northwindsystems.com>
- **Process**:
  - Use the data transfer tool to securely share all necessary files with the employee's successor or designated person.
    - Log in as Offboarding Team Lead.
    - Navigate to "Data Transfer".
    - Create a new transfer request and specify the recipient(s).
    - Upload relevant documents (e.g., project reports, meeting notes) and assign them to the appropriate recipients.

### 4. Document Review and Archiving
- **Tool Name**: Document Management System: <https://docs.northwindsystems.com>
- **Process**:
  - Ensure all important company documents are reviewed for any sensitive information.
    - Log in as Offboarding Team Lead.
    - Navigate to "Document Management".
    - Select the relevant document library and review contents.
  - Archive or delete unnecessary files.
    - For archived files, ensure they are properly labeled with the employee’s name and date of archiving.

### 5. Final Confirmation
- **Tool Name**: Offboarding Checklist: <https://offboarding.northwindsystems.com/checklist>
- **Process**:
  - Complete the final offboarding checklist to confirm that all steps have been executed.
    - Log in as Offboarding Team Lead or HR representative.
    - Navigate to "Offboarding Checklist".
    - Check and confirm each task has been completed.

### 6. Follow-Up
- **Tool Name**: Internal Communication Tool: <https://comm.northwindsystems.com>
- **Process**:
  - Send a follow-up email to the employee’s manager and HR representative confirming that the offboarding process is complete.
    - Include any outstanding tasks or reminders for future reference.

## Troubleshooting

### Issue: Employee's SSO Access Not Revoked
- **Resolution**: 
  - Verify the correct user account was selected in the SSO portal.
  - If still unresolved, contact IT Support at <ITSupport@northwindsystems.com> for assistance.

### Issue: Data Transfer Tool Issues
- **Resolution**:
  - Check internet connectivity and ensure all files are correctly uploaded.
  - If issues persist, reach out to the Data Management Team at <datamanagementteam@northwindsystems.com> for support.

### Issue: Missing Items in Offboarding Checklist
- **Resolution**:
  - Review the checklist template to identify any missing items.
  - Contact HR or IT directly if further assistance is needed.

## Conclusion
Following this SOP ensures a smooth and secure offboarding process, safeguarding company data and maintaining compliance. Regular updates to this document should be made based on feedback and changing company policies.
